## A little about me

I'm a freelance software engineer building websites for clients and teams who want their products to make a difference in people's lives.

My path through **security operations** and **social service** shaped how I work: listen closely, communicate clearly, and solve problems around people's real needs. Today, I bring that perspective to the web experiences I build.

- **Currently building:** an application that helps lower-income parents and mothers find free or low-cost newborn essentials, including clothing, car seats, and other basics.
- **Open to:** freelance website projects and connecting with teams building meaningful products.
- **Pronouns:** she/her/hers.
- **Off-screen:** staying active, volunteering, and rewatching *Chappie*.
